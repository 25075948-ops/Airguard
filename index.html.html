    About the data

    AirGuard aggregates air quality data from multiple sources including the World Air Quality Index (WAQI) platform and Malaysia's Department of Environment APIMS system. Data is updated hourly and represents the official AQI calculation based on six pollutants: PM2.5, PM10, O₃, NO₂, SO₂, and CO.

    Official DOE APIMS ↗ Ministry of Health ↗ WAQI API Docs ↗ AQICN Malaysia ↗
